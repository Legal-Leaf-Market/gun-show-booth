# Name ideas

One umbrella brand that works at gun shows, Fountain Square pop-ups, flea markets, and whatever you both sell next (coasters, baked goods, merch). None of them say "firearms," so nobody walks up expecting an FFL.

| # | Name | Why it works |
|---|------|--------------|
| 1 | **Hot Creek Trading Co.** | Your original idea minus "Firearms." "Trading Co." covers anything you put on the table. |
| 2 | **Mule Train Mercantile** | Old-West freight wagon on the surface. "Mule" is the smuggling wink for the people who catch it. |
| 3 | **Rumrunner Supply Co.** | Prohibition-era smuggler. Outlaw, but the kind your grandpa tells stories about. |
| 4 | **The Plug Trading Co.** | "The plug" is slang for your connect. Gangster, but mainstream enough that nobody blinks. |
| 5 | **Dead Drop Supply** | Spy-movie handoff. Fits "text us and we'll hold it for pickup at the show." |
| 6 | **Smokewagon Goods** | "Smoke wagon" is old cowboy slang for a revolver. The gun crowd gets it without you selling guns. |
| 7 | **Side Hustle Syndicate** | "Syndicate" brings the mob energy, "side hustle" covers coasters, cookies and whatever's next. |
| 8 | **Booth Bandits** | Playful and works at any event, not just gun shows. |
| 9 | **Bootlegger's Table** | Indiana Prohibition flavor, and "table" is literally what you run. |
| 10 | **Contraband Co.** | The edgiest one. Reads as a joke, but police get in free at these shows, so expect to hear about it. |

Changing the name later takes one edit: the Business tab on the phone admin page, or `name` in `data/site.json`. Domain and trademark availability haven't been checked yet.
