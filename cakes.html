Made for your occasion

Birthday cakes, baby showers, weddings or just because. Choose round or sheet cakes, vanilla, chocolate or marble, with buttercream or whipped icing. Other flavors are available on request. Final pricing depends on size, flavor and design.

How ordering works

  1. Choose a cake or cupcakes, then fill in the size, date, flavor and design.
  2. Prepare your request. Send it by text, or copy it into a message to CJ's Cakes on Facebook.
  3. We reply to confirm availability, the final price, pickup or delivery details, and payment arrangements. Preparing a request does not reserve a date or place an order.

Ask us about lead time for your date. Delivery availability, any delivery charge, pickup timing and payment arrangements are confirmed with your request.

Pickup and delivery around Columbus

We offer delivery or curbside pickup around Columbus, Seymour, Greensburg, North Vernon, Vernon, Hope, Edinburgh, Nashville and Bloomington. Ask about your address when requesting delivery.

For show pickup, choose a listed show and pickup day on a regular product. We confirm what we can bring; the catalog does not guarantee stock at every event.
