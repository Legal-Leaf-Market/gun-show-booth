When to call (our judgement, not a published rule):

- **Tuesday to Thursday, mid-morning or early afternoon.** Promoters spend
  Friday setting up, and Saturday and Sunday running a show. CIGS runs a
  show almost every weekend somewhere in Indiana.
- **Not during a show.** Show hours are 9am to 5pm Saturday and 9am to 3pm
  Sunday. A call then lands at a busy door table.
- **CIGS has two people and an email.** If Dan doesn't call back in two
  days, try Brandy, and email cigshows@gmail.com so the answer is in writing.
- **Straight Shooters has one person.** One Straight Shooters listing spells
  the email "straightshootersnterprises" (missing an e). That is a typo, so
  use the spelling in the table.

## The one question that matters most

CIGS's vendor rules, as the listings quote them, say **vendors must sell
gun or knife show related items**. That runs Stout Field, Greensburg,
Richmond, Crown Point and Evansville. Ask Dan directly whether sealed,
labeled home-baked goods count, before any other question. A no here
affects five shows.

Other CIGS rules from the same page:

- Vendors stay **both days**.
- **5 days' notice** to cancel a table.
- 8 ft table and chairs provided, electricity included (Stout Field, $50).
- Dealer setup Friday 2pm to 8pm. The general vendor page also lists
  Saturday 7am to 9am, but that may not apply at every venue. Ask.
