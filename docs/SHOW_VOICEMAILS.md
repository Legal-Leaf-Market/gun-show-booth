# Voicemails for the November and December 2026 shows

Scripts to leave when an organizer doesn't pick up, one per show in
`docs/SHOW_LEADS.md`. Written 2026-11-02. Each runs about 30 seconds.
Fill in `[your name]` before you call. Everything a script says comes from
the leads sheet; the fees and dates there are the research report's numbers,
not the organizer's, so the voicemails ask rather than assume.

Tips that apply to every call:

- Say the phone number slowly, and say it twice. (812) 561-0549 is the one
  number for Booth Bandits and CJ's Cakes.
- Lead with "baked goods", because that is the question most likely to get a
  no (kitchen and concession contracts). Better to hear it on the callback
  than at load-in.
- If they call back, the three questions on the leads sheet are the
  checklist: home-baked goods allowed, insurance certificate, teardown time.
- After a show says yes, add it from the phone admin (Events tab) with its own
  admission price.

Not on this list, on purpose:

- **Nov 14–15 and Dec 12–13** are booked per the owner, but no lead matches
  them, so there is no organizer to call. Which shows these are is still an
  open question for the owner.
- **Broad Ripple Winter Farmers Market** books through MarketSpread, not by
  phone. Apply online. Open Saturdays that don't clash with a booked weekend:
  Nov 7, Nov 21 and Dec 26.
